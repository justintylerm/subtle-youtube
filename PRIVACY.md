# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 30, 2026

Subtle for YouTube is designed to work entirely in your browser.

## Data collection

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, browsing history, video history, account details, analytics, or telemetry.

## Local storage

The extension uses Chrome's `storage` permission only to remember display preferences such as your selected feed view, grayscale choice, and Grid-view toggles. These settings remain in the browser profile where the extension is installed.

## Site access

Access to `https://www.youtube.com/*` is used only to change the visual presentation of the YouTube Subscriptions page. The extension does not authenticate with Google or YouTube and does not make requests to a developer-controlled server.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be documented in the repository and included in a release.

## Contact

Questions can be opened as a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/justintylerm/subtle-youtube/issues).
